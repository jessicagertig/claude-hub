Consequence: one false conversion row + false PostHog conversion event per existing paying subscription over the first billing cycle after deploy (~1 month monthly, up to ~12 months annual), plus a false Discord "Trial Converted to Paid! 🎉🚀" ping (`Discord::NotifyTrialConvertedToPaidJob`) for each trial-era customer. D5's "renewals classify correctly for free" holds only for subscriptions whose ledger history begins before their first cash. No DB-local discriminator exists inside D5's ruled predicate (`stripe_subscription_status` is 'active' both at a trial conversion and at a renewal — the exact ambiguity that mis-times today's Discord ping; `billing_reason` is deliberately unused per D5).
