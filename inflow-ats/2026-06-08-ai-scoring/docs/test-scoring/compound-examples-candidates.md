# Compound Example Candidates from 180 JDs

Sentences that contain multiple genuinely distinct requirements. For use as decomposition examples in Call 2 and Call 2b.

## Strong candidates

1. "Excellent written and oral communication skills in English, proficiency with word processing software such as Google Docs or Microsoft Office, and an ability to independently conceptualise and write comprehensive reports on the state of ongoing work whenever requested"
   - Communication skills + software proficiency + report writing — three different things

2. "Strong proficiency in TypeScript and Node.js; experience with Next.js and React a plus"
   - Required skill + nice-to-have skill at different tiers, joined by semicolon

3. "A Bachelor's degree in Information Science, Computer Science or similar discipline, with 5+ years of application development and 2 years of prior lead experience or instrumentation or 8+ years of equivalent work experience."
   - Degree + development experience + lead experience — three distinct qualifications

4. "Write protocols, prepare data presentations for company-wide meetings, maintain a highly organized and detailed electronic laboratory notebook (ELN) and stay up to date with scientific literature"
   - Protocol writing + presentations + notebook maintenance + lit review — four unrelated tasks

5. "You must also successfully approach potential customers, manage the sales cycle, close deals, negotiate contracts, and impart a level of comfort to end-users and technology departments on the benefits of products and services."
   - Prospecting + sales cycle + closing + negotiation + evangelism

6. "Lead, manage, and hold accountable teams and directors in areas including, but not limited to, client compensation, transitions, trading, client service and brokerage operations."
   - Leadership/management + multiple distinct domain areas

## Borderline — probably should NOT decompose

7. "Build strong and genuine relationships with our clients, maintaining regular contact to keep them updated on overall project progress, highlighting any potential delays or scope creep so that a solution can be agreed upon mutually."
   - One thing (client relationship management) described in detail

8. "Maintain and repair smoke and heat detectors, magnetic door holders, and fire alarm system components, including circuit cards, power supplies, software, copper and fiber optic systems platforms."
   - One thing (maintenance/repair) applied to a list of related components

9. "Comfort operating within a startup environment, including the ability to quickly get up to speed on new content areas, multi-task, and adjust your facilitation style to accommodate different individuals."
   - One thing (startup adaptability) with elaboration
