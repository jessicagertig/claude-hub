## Criteria

1. [tier_2] Develop and implement customer service policies and procedures.
   Full match examples: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) design for CX; Creation of Service Level Agreement (SLA) frameworks; Authoring of Quality Assurance (QA) scorecards
   Partial match examples: Business process re-engineering; Policy compliance auditing; User manual documentation

2. [tier_2] Monitor customer service representatives’ performance and provide ongoing coaching and feedback.
   Full match examples: Conducting CSAT/NPS deep-dive performance reviews; Quality monitoring (QM) calibration sessions; One-on-one coaching for First Call Resolution (FCR) improvement
   Partial match examples: Employee performance appraisal (general); On-the-job training oversight; Call center metric tracking

3. [tier_2] Resolve customer complaints and inquiries in a timely and efficient manner.
   Full match examples: Tier 3 escalation handling; Service Recovery process management; Root Cause Analysis (RCA) for client disputes
   Partial match examples: Conflict mediation; Client relationship retention management; Stakeholder grievance handling

4. [tier_2] Analyze customer service trends and recommend improvements.
   Full match examples: Customer Journey Mapping analysis; Voice of the Customer (VoC) report interpretation; Churn reduction strategy analysis
   Partial match examples: Data-driven operational efficiency reporting; Market trend research; Consumer behavior pattern analysis

5. [tier_2] Train and onboard new customer service representatives.
   Full match examples: Facilitating product knowledge transfer sessions; Managing Learning Management System (LMS) curriculum for support; New hire nesting supervision
   Partial match examples: Corporate onboarding program facilitation; Instructional design for internal training; Mentorship program coordination

6. [tier_2] Monitor customer service representatives’ adherence to company policies and procedures.
   Full match examples: Compliance auditing for support workflows; Adherence tracking to workforce management schedules; Internal support audit documentation
   Partial match examples: Regulatory compliance monitoring; Policy enforcement oversight; Internal process auditing

7. [tier_2] Ensure customer service representatives maintain a high level of customer service.
   Full match examples: CX Quality Assurance oversight; Maintaining CSAT/CES benchmarks; Brand voice consistency audits
   Partial match examples: Service excellence initiatives; Client satisfaction program management; Quality culture promotion

8. [tier_2] Prepare and analyze customer service reports.
   Full match examples: Dashboard creation in Zendesk Explore or Salesforce Analytics; WFM (Workforce Management) reporting; Monthly support volume and efficiency analysis
   Partial match examples: Operational business intelligence reporting; Data visualization for management; Performance metric dashboarding

9. [tier_2] Foster an environment of collaboration and teamwork among customer service representatives.
   Full match examples: Managing knowledge base cross-functional collaboration; Facilitating peer-to-peer feedback loops; Team-based support incentive programs
