# What's New: Plato AI

Meet **Plato**, your AI hiring assistant. Plato reads a candidate's resume, measures it against the requirements in your job description, and gives you a clear fit score, a plain-language summary, and a criteria-by-criteria breakdown, so you can spend your time on the strongest people instead of the resume pile.

You'll find Plato in three places:

- **On any candidate** — the new **Plato** tab in the candidate profile.
- **Across a whole job** — the **Run Plato** card on the job's pipeline.
- **In your account** — **Account → Plato AI**, where admins manage settings, usage, and billing.

Here's what shipped.

---

## Automatic reviews for every new applicant

Turn Plato on once and let it work in the background. When automatic reviews are enabled, every new applicant with a resume — on a job that has a description, and while you have credits — is reviewed as they come in, so the moment you open a candidate their fit score is already waiting.

- **Set it and forget it.** Enable **Auto-generate Plato reviews for new applicants** in **Account → Plato AI → Settings** (admins only).
- **Override it per job.** Some roles need it, some don't. Each job has its own **Plato AI settings** tab in Job Setup where you can turn automatic reviews on or off for that job specifically, or leave it to follow your account default.
- **Runs quietly.** Automatic reviews happen in the background with no interruptions or pop-ups. Each successful review uses one credit.
- **Also new in Settings:** choose whether hiring-team members (not just admins) can spend credits, and set up **low-balance and empty-balance email alerts** with your own threshold so you're never caught short.

## Review a single candidate on demand

Want Plato's read on one person right now? Open their **Plato** tab and click **Generate review**. Plato processes the resume, scores it against the role, and shows you the finished review.

- **A live progress view** walks you through each step (reading the resume, analyzing the candidate, scoring against the role, finalizing) and tells you you're free to keep working while Plato finishes.
- **You'll get a notification** the moment the review is done.
- **Keep reviews fresh.** If a candidate uploads a new resume, their existing review is marked out of date and you can **regenerate** it with one click. Plato reminds you it uses one credit and confirms before spending it, and your previous review stays visible until the new one is ready.
- **No charge for failures.** If a review can't be completed, no credit is used and you're told to try again.

## Review a whole job at once

New hires don't come one at a time, and neither does Plato. Two bulk options let you score many candidates in a single action.

- **Review the entire job.** The **Run Plato** card on a job's pipeline reviews candidates across every stage in one go. If the job still needs a description or has no candidates yet, Plato tells you what to do first instead of running on empty.
- **Review one stage or a hand-picked group.** From a stage's candidate list, use **Generate AI summaries** to review everyone in that stage, or check off just the candidates you want.
- **Know the cost before you commit.** Every bulk screen shows how many candidates will be reviewed and how many credits you have. If you don't have enough, a clear heads-up appears before you run anything.
- **Get the results by email.** When a bulk run finishes, you'll receive an email with the final count of reviews completed, alongside an in-app confirmation.

## Skip — or re-review — candidates who already have a score

Bulk runs are smart about credits. By default, Plato **skips candidates who already have a completed review**, so re-running a bulk action never double-charges you for work already done, and it doesn't count those candidates against your total.

- **Save credits automatically.** Candidates that already have a review are left out of the count, and the number of candidates to be reviewed updates live as you choose your options.
- **Re-score when you need to.** Changed the job description, or want a fresh look? When you review the whole job with **Run Plato**, turn on the **re-score** option to include already-reviewed candidates and generate new reviews for them. (The per-stage **Generate AI summaries** action always skips candidates who already have a review.)
- **Filter to exactly who you mean.** The candidate list has a new **Filter by fit** control. Narrow to a fit band, or to **Not yet scored** candidates, then Select-All — and Plato (as well as bulk message and bulk move) acts only on the filtered group.

## See fit at a glance, everywhere

Plato surfaces its results wherever you're already working.

- **In the candidate list**, each person shows a fit-band label (such as "Good fit") with a small grayscale fill indicator, or a "review in progress" indicator while Plato is still working.
- **On the Plato tab**, the full review includes an overall fit score and rating (from poor to excellent, with stars), a headline take, a "Fit for this role" narrative, and an expandable breakdown of how the candidate did on your **core**, **preferred**, and **bonus** criteria — showing what was met, partially met, or missing. It also calls out notable achievements, relevant experience, open questions to explore, and the candidate's key skills.
- **In the candidate's activity timeline**, a Plato review callout gives you the headline and score with a link straight to the full review.

## Manage credits and billing

Plato reviews run on credits, and there's a home for managing them under **Account → Plato AI**.

- **Buy credits your way** — a recurring subscription for steady hiring, or a one-off top-up when you need a boost.
- **See exactly what you have.** The Usage view breaks your balance down by source — monthly plan credits, subscription credits, and top-up credits — and shows when each resets.
- **Never run dry unexpectedly.** Optional low-balance and empty-balance email alerts keep admins informed, and your balance refreshes automatically after a purchase.
