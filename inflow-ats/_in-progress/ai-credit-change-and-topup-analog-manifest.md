# AI Credit Billing — Change & Top-Up Analog Manifest

Purpose: a structure-first, row-by-row diff of the AI-credit subscription-change and one-off top-up flows against their shipped analogs, grounded only in current working-tree code.

Branch: `ai-feature-work-v5`. All billing work in this manifest is uncommitted in the working tree.

---

## Two analogs named

- **Subscription change** (AI-credit tier upgrade/downgrade): analog is the **Stripe Billing Portal redirect** — `BillingController#change_subscription_portal_session`. The controller persists nothing; reconciliation is deferred to the inbound Stripe webhook.
- **One-off top-up** (direct credit-pack charge): analog is **We Work Remotely listing purchase** — `BoardWwrListing#charge_for_listing`. A carrier record is pre-created, charged via a model method, and the inbound webhook finalizes the existing record found by a metadata id.

**Summary:** OURS does NOT match the analog. (2) MISSING — no post-grant notification job (WWR enqueues `PaidWwrListingCreatedJob`); not forced. (3) EXTRA — `PurchaseAiCreditTopUpConfirmModal.tsx`, a confirm dialog WWR never had; not forced. Headline finding: the intermediate `stripe_invoice_paid` payment-confirmed marker is genuinely MISSING and that absence is not forced by the data model.
