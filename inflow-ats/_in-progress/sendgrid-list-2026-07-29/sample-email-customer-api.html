We're excited to announce the Polymer Customer API, giving you authenticated access to your organization's hiring data for building integrations, automating workflows, and powering AI-driven hiring tools.

Full docs here: developer.polymer.co

What you can do with it:

  • Read your data programmatically. Pull jobs, candidates, applications, comments, reviews, messages, and hiring stage history.
  • Submit applications via API. Use the Apply endpoint for full applications or Import for sourced candidates.
  • Move candidates through your pipeline. Advance or move back candidates programmatically, with control over message automations.
  • Write comments and reviews. Create, update, and delete comments and reviews on applications. Ideal for writing AI evaluations back into Polymer.
  • Export resumes in bulk. Download all resumes for any job as a ZIP file.

Only admins can generate API keys. If you're not an admin, reach out to one on your team to get started.
