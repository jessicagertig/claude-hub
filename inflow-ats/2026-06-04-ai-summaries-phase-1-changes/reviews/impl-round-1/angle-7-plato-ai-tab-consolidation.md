# Plato AI Tab Consolidation — Round 1

## Findings

- F1 [HIGH] `app/javascript/ats/src/views/accountAdmin/accountPlatoAi/AccountPlatoAiContainer.tsx:48-53` + `AccountContainer.tsx:207` / `OrganizationAiSettings` component destructures `currentOrganization` from its props (line 17: `function OrganizationAiSettings({ setIsDirty, currentOrganization })`) and immediately accesses `currentOrganization.settings` (line 18). Previously, `AccountContainer` explicitly passed `currentOrganization={currentOrganization}` when rendering `OrganizationAiSettings`. Now the route renders `<AccountPlatoAiContainer {...props} {...renderProps} />`, and `AccountPlatoAiContainer` passes `{...props} {...renderProps} setIsDirty={setIsDirty}` to `OrganizationAiSettings`. But `currentOrganization` is NOT on `props` -- it comes from `useCurrentSession()` inside `AccountContainer` (line 33). The `...props` spread from `AccountContainer`'s render prop only contains React Router props (`history`, `location`, `match`, `loading`). `currentOrganization` will be `undefined` when `OrganizationAiSettings` tries to access it, causing a runtime crash: `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'settings')`. / Recommended fix: Either (a) have `AccountPlatoAiContainer` call `useCurrentSession()` itself and pass `currentOrganization` to `OrganizationAiSettings`, or (b) have `AccountContainer` pass `currentOrganization` as a prop to `AccountPlatoAiContainer` and have the container forward it.
