Open Application to Talent Collectives

Talent Collectives are invite or application only groups of professionals who are either passively or actively looking for new opportunities.

Regardless of where you are in your job search, you can fill out the information below for a chance to receive curated introduction requests from top-tier companies.

Note that the collective receives intro requests on your behalf and delivers them directly to your inbox. That means you will not get any outreach from recruiters without your direct approval.

If you want to remain anonymous, just indicate that below and we will redact any personally identifiable info from the profiles we share with companies.

All you have to do is mark what you're looking for and we'll send you an email if your application is accepted.

If you have any questions don't hesitate to email team@pallet.xyz
