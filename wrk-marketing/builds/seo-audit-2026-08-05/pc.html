Our Story

Reinventing the world of legacy supply chain systems

The global supply chain powers economies and underpins all human needs. We started Pallet because too much of supply chain still runs on legacy systems and tribal knowledge.

We're building tools that automate freight operations — processing shipments, connecting the dots, and streamlining partner communication. Our vision is a smarter, faster supply chain that drives a more prosperous future.
