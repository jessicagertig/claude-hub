## COMPETITOR ENTRY NOTES (verified research, August 2026)

### Breezy (keep, existing entry)
- **Angle: Best for paid niche job board integrations.**
- The current article's angle ("access to their candidate database with millions of job seekers") describes a feature that does not exist. Breezy has no pre-existing candidate sourcing database; its "candidate database" is the customer's own applicants. Replace the angle entirely.
- The angle replaces the old one entirely: **40+ paid niche job board integrations available.** Positive framing only.
- **Provenance, so nobody re-derives it: Jessica counted the paid niche board integrations inside the Breezy app.** That list is not published anywhere on breezy.hr. `breezy.hr/integrations` is a marketing page covering all integrations, not the paid niche job boards, and enumerating it produces a different and unstable number — three separate counts of it returned 44, 51 and a third figure, none of them what the app shows. **Do not verify this figure against a public page and do not drop it for lacking a public source. It is a direct observation of the product.**
- The figure is written **40+** rather than a precise count, which is accurate under her app count and under every public enumeration, all of which came back at or above 40.
- Pricing: Startup plan $157/month annual, $189/month monthly. Higher tiers: Growth $273/month annual, Business $439/month annual.
- AI: "Breezy Intelligence" — candidate scoring (Applicant Insights), interview note summaries (Activity Summary), AI-resume fraud detection (Resume Audit). Sold as a paid add-on at $30 per 100,000 credits; AI features stop when credits run out. Not included with plans.
- Bulk actions: bulk email and bulk candidate actions on paid plans.
- Career page: branded career sites included on all plans including free tier.
- Do not mention: job posting approval timing, Indeed, distribution counts, or anything about their "immediately" distribution claim.

### Recooty (keep, existing entry — needs significant update)
- **Angle: Best for busy teams with standard hiring processes (keep the existing set-and-forget automation angle; it is still accurate).**
- The article's current pricing ($49/month Pro) is dead. Current: Starter $79/month annual (5 active job posts, 2,000 AI credits/month), Standard $149/month annual (unlimited job posts, 5,000 AI credits/month, bulk candidate actions), Premier custom quote.
- Now heavily AI-marketed: AI job descriptions, AI candidate matching, AI resume parsing, AI scoring. Claims distribution to 250+ job boards including Indeed, LinkedIn, Google for Jobs.
- Career page: their builder produces a hosted page assembled from logo, company description, photos/videos, and an auto-updating job list. Their own guide describes it as "as simple as changing your Instagram bio." Their marketing additionally claims font/color/styling choices and custom domain hosting. Describe it as a hosted careers page with branding customization; do not call it a "no-code builder."
- Has an embeddable job board widget (their customer showcase documents this).
- Bulk actions: gated to Standard plan and up. State this specifically.

### Zoho Recruit (keep, existing entry)
- **Angle: Best for top-quality customizations (keep the existing angle; it is still accurate).**
- Pricing: per user, per month, two editions. Corporate HR: Standard ~$25/user/month annual, Enterprise ~$50. Staffing Agency edition adds Professional tier ($25/$50/$75). Monthly billing runs roughly 20% higher. Do not print job limits for Zoho; their pricing unit is seats, not jobs.
- Free plan exists: 1 active job, no AI, no integrations, no resume parsing.
- AI: Zia — candidate matching, Sourcing Bot, Screening Bot, AI Assessments (October 2025 release). Included with paid plans at no additional cost.
- Bulk actions: mass email, mass update, bulk status changes. Daily mass-email limits by edition: Standard 250/day, Professional 500/day, Enterprise 750/day, purchasable up to 2,250/day. No mass email on the free edition.
- Career page: configuration to match branding; CSS/styling control on higher tiers.

### Tracker (keep, existing entry)
- **Angle: Best for an all-in-one CRM and ATS solution (keep the existing angle; it is still accurate).**
- Full name TrackerRMS. Still sold as a combined ATS + CRM, agency/staffing-oriented, quote-only, sales-led.
- AI: "AI Content Suite" (one-click emails, job posts, screening questions, candidate summaries) and AI candidate ranking engine, listed as included checkmarks on their plans page. Announced EVA conversational AI agent (March 2025).
- Career page: "Jobs+" — a career microsite built and hosted by Tracker as a supplemental paid service on a subdomain of the customer's site. For the table this was flattened to "Yes."
- Has a REST API with interactive Swagger documentation (released July 2025).

### JazzHR (new entry — replaces SpringRecruit)
- **Angle: Best for compliance and hiring reports.**
- SpringRecruit is removed: near-dormant product, undermines list credibility.
- Source all claims from jazzhr.com. Their own emphasis: data-driven insights, recruiting bottleneck reports, hiring spend, compliance, audit trails, ISO 27001, payroll/HR integrations (BambooHR, UKG).
- Do not highlight knockout questions or screening automation in JazzHR's entry.
- Pricing: Hero $75/month annual ($99 monthly) with 3 active jobs and $9/month per additional job; Plus $269/month annual; Pro $420/month annual. Their website anchors on annual pricing with a "save by paying annually" framing; monthly pricing is not independently published and monthly plans cannot be started self-serve. In the table this is "Annual pricing published."
- AI: "TalentFit" resume-to-job matching. Not included on Hero; available on Plus plan and up (two independent pricing breakdowns agree).
- Bulk actions: bulk email, bulk reject, bulk status change, bulk move between jobs. Restricted to Super Admins, Recruiting Admins, and Super Users. Bulk email cap raised only by contacting them.
- Career page: hosted careers page; brand-specific customization sold as an add-on service performed by their team.
- Has a partner-oriented API (application submission, candidate export events).
- Owned by Employ (same parent as Lever).

### Lever (keep — reframe angle)
- **Angle: Best for teams planning to scale into high-volume hiring.**
- The angle replaces the old customer-service one. Do not frame Lever as a small-business tool.
- Pricing: custom quote only, sales-led. No published pricing.
- AI: Talent Fit matching, AI screening support, AI Interview Companion (via Employ's AI).
- Bulk actions: bulk action toolbar (stage moves, archiving with email, tagging, bulk email) available to all roles across all packages.
- Career page: Lever's hosted Job Site supports logo, color changes, and content (values, mission, benefits) — their own product page claims a "fully branded experience." Custom careers sites are commonly built by customers using Lever's Postings API.
- Lever's API includes an "Apply to a Posting" endpoint accepting full applications including custom question responses and a confirmation-email parameter. This is reflected in Lever's "Further job board customization" cell as "Custom sites via API" and should not be expanded further.
